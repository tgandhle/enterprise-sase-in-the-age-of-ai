Review checklist

Generated from the reference sections of each topic page by tools/build_checklist.py. Do not edit this file by hand. 5 items, sorted by severity. Ticks are not saved.

Each item carries a default severity: Critical, High or Medium. The default answers one question: what does losing this one control allow, with no credit for any other control that makes the same decision? Critical: one request, or one piece of content an attacker supplies, directly sends the organization's data or a credential somewhere not authorized to receive it, lets a person, device or agent reach what it should not, takes over an identity or a credential, runs code from outside, or causes a consequential action nobody authorized. High: the same outcome needs exactly one thing first (a stolen credential, a position on the network path or inside the network, a victim's action, a trusted party such as a contracted vendor, or an ordinary fault or honest mistake), or needs only repeated attempts, or the missing control allows unbounded cost or loss of availability. Medium: anything lower that still has a security effect. An item that detects or recovers is High when no other item does that job for a Critical-class event, and Medium otherwise. A test, review or record is rated one level below the control it assures. The full rubric is in CONVENTIONS.md in the repository.

The default is a starting point and not the severity of a finding. Rate a finding in your own review by what the traffic exposes, who can reach it and what else stands in the way. An item that does not apply to your design is not a finding.
